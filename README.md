## About Me

I'm a 17-year-old **Systems Development student at SENAI**, passionate about technology, robotics, design and software development.

I'm part of the **ARMADILLOS robotics team**, where I work mainly with programming and technical development. Throughout my journey in competitive robotics, I have been **2× Regional OBR Champion**, qualified for the **OBR State Stage twice**, and had the opportunity to compete at the **RoboCup in Canada**.

I enjoy turning ideas into real projects — from **software and web systems to robots, automation and digital experiences**.

Currently, I'm constantly learning and exploring new technologies, combining **development, creativity and problem-solving** to build better solutions.

> **Building ideas. Creating solutions. Exploring what's next.**
